Signature Events

Partnership Lake Houston is committed to helping businesses connect, protect, and grow in their communities. One of the ways we accomplish this is by hosting events throughout the year that provide opportunities for networking, economic growth, and leadership development.

  • Partnership Golf Classic

    The Annual Chamber Classic has been Lake Houston area’s premier business golf tournament for nearly three decades. Held at Golf Club of Houston, the Golf Classic offers our sponsors tremendous marketing exposure, and an incomparable day of golf on a course that has consistently been ranked among the top in the state. This event provides the perfect venue for players to connect with business owners and community members, to win great prizes and to enjoy outstanding food while experiencing course exclusivity during the tournament.

  • The Annual Chairman's Ball

    The Annual Chairman's Ball is a prestigious event that honors the year's outstanding volunteer leaders and their achievements. The evening features the installation of the new Board Chair and board of directors, along with the presentation of esteemed awards such as the Dr. Hayden E. McKay M.D. Citizen of the Year Award, the Spirit of the Chamber Award, and the Rising Star Award.

  • Party Lake Houston

    ​Party Lake Houston is an annual event hosted by Partnership Lake Houston that brings together local businesses and community members for an evening of lively entertainment and great food. This event offers attendees a chance to connect with fellow supporters of the Lake Houston area. The festivities include activities such as racecar simulators and raffles with incredible prizes.

Monthly Luncheons

We’re dedicated to becoming your one-stop premier resource for information and business best practices within our local community. The listed events below are some of the communities' favorites. Attend a monthly luncheon to hear from a variety of dynamic speakers and industry experts. Click on the below text to read about some of the communities’ favorite events. For more information about the Partnership luncheons, click here.

Small Business

The Small Business Luncheon is a dynamic event celebrating the innovation and impact of small businesses in our community. This luncheon provides an excellent opportunity for networking, learning, and collaboration among local entrepreneurs and business leaders. Featuring an engaging keynote speaker and valuable insights on topics relevant to small business success, this event is a must-attend for those looking to grow and thrive in today’s competitive market.

Teacher of the Year

The Teacher of the Year Luncheon is a vibrant celebration of excellence in education, honoring exceptional teachers from Humble ISD, Sheldon ISD, local private schools, San Jacinto College - Generation Park, and Lone Star College - Kingwood. It’s a heartwarming opportunity for the business community to come together and show appreciation for the incredible impact these educators have on our students and the future of our community. Join us as we shine a spotlight on their dedication and achievements!

Impact of Houston Sports

The Impact of Houston Sports event, hosted by Partnership Lake Houston, will explore the powerful influence of sports on our local economy, community engagement, and business growth. From professional teams to youth athletics, sports play a vital role in shaping Houston’s identity, driving tourism, and creating opportunities for businesses of all sizes.

Join us as we hear from industry leaders and sports executives who will share insights on the economic impact, brand partnerships, and the future of sports in our region. This event is perfect for business professionals, sports enthusiasts, and community leaders looking to understand how Houston’s dynamic sports scene fuels innovation and economic development.

Leadership Awards

Partnership Lake Houston is thrilled to honor the exceptional professionals and Leadership Lake Houston alumni who are making a lasting impact on our community. These inspiring individuals embody excellence in every aspect of their lives, showcasing remarkable leadership in both business and community, while also achieving a dynamic balance between career success and personal fulfillment. Their dedication, hard work, and passion are shaping the future of our community, and we are proud to celebrate their outstanding contributions!

First Responders Appreciation

We are beyond excited to celebrate and honor the incredible First Responders of the Lake Houston Area at this special luncheon! It’s a chance to come together as a community and salute the everyday heroes who serve and protect us with unwavering courage and dedication. Local First Responders will be recognized for their bravery and the sacrifices they make each day. Join us as we express our deepest gratitude and appreciation for these remarkable individuals who make our community safer and stronger! Let’s make this a truly unforgettable event in their honor!

Women In Business

The Women in Business Luncheon is an exciting celebration of women leaders and professionals from the Lake Houston Area! This event will provide a unique opportunity for businesswomen to connect, share experiences, and strengthen their professional relationships, all while fostering a supportive and inclusive environment. We have a dynamic and inspiring guest speaker and will witness the presentation of the prestigious “ATHENA” Leadership Award, recognizing a local businesswoman for her exceptional leadership and success. It’s a day of empowerment, connection, and celebration you won’t want to miss!

Veterans Appreciation

Join us for a truly special event as we come together to honor and thank the brave men and women who have served our nation. The Veterans Appreciation Luncheon is a heartfelt occasion to recognize the sacrifice, courage, and commitment of our veterans. Let’s unite in gratitude and celebration of their extraordinary service and dedication to our country.

State & Federal Outlook

​The State & Federal Outlook Luncheon, hosted by Partnership Lake Houston, is an annual event that brings together community members to discuss key governmental issues and policies affecting the region. In past events, notable figures such as Congressman Dan Crenshaw have served as guest speakers, providing valuable insights into federal matters impacting the community.  Attendees have the opportunity to engage directly with policymakers, fostering a deeper understanding of legislative developments at both state and federal levels.​
