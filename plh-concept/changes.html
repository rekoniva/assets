Website Audit & Redesign: Partnership Lake Houston

This page sums up the concept redesign of lakehouston.org and a quick audit of the chamber’s website, Facebook Page and Google Business Profile. Start with the top 5 fixes, then open any section for the details.

Top 5 fixes

  1. Urgent Bizcom sponsorship email is misspelled (LakeHosuton.org), so emails won’t reach the chamber.
  2. Urgent Industries tiles open Word documents in a personal OneDrive folder.
  3. Urgent The Volunteer page is a 404 (“Page not found”).
  4. Fix Homepage event errors: “11:0 AM” and CDT instead of CST.
  5. Urgent Facebook is missing the phone number and hours, and engagement is low.

Key: Urgent broken or missing Fix wrong or weak Polish nice to tidy

2. Website fixes

9 items, 3 urgent. Broken links, wrong times, typos

Listed by impact: broken links first, then wrong information, then typos.

Broken: messages or visitors don’t get through

  • Urgent Bizcom sponsorship email is misspelled. On lakehouston.org/bizcom/ the line reads “Sponsorships are available by emailing interest to SMagee@LakeHosuton.org”. “Hosuton” isn’t the chamber’s domain, so those emails won’t reach you. The correct address, smagee@lakehouston.org, is on the very next line.
  • Urgent Industries tiles open Word documents in a personal OneDrive folder. On lakehouston.org/industries/, all 5 tiles (Energy Technology, Life Sciences, Logistics, Advanced Manufacturing, Corporate Operations) link to .docx files on a personal OneDrive account rather than to web pages. Visitors may be asked to sign in.
  • Urgent The Volunteer page is missing. lakehouston.org/volunteer/ shows “Page not found.”

Wrong information

  • Fix Veterans Appreciation Luncheon time. The homepage (lakehouston.org/) reads “11:0 AM - 1:00 PM (CST)”. The event calendar has it right: 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM.
  • Fix Sheldon Bizcom time zone. The homepage lists Thursday, November 12 as “11:00 AM - 12:00 PM (CDT)”. Daylight saving time ends November 1, so this should read CST.

Typos and tidying

  • Polish Our Elite Investors, Generation Park (lakehouston.org/our-elite-investors/): “spend countries hours” → “spend countless hours”; “enable those of our clients” → “enable our clients”; “For its the employees” → “For it’s the employees”.
  • Polish Our Elite Investors, Memorial Hermann: “for 40 years Recognized” is missing a period after “years.”
  • Polish Testimonials (lakehouston.org/testimonials/): “priceless. .” has an extra period.
  • Polish Member Directory categories. The directory (members.lakehouston.org/active-member-directory) has 106 categories. 56 of them have only 1 or 2 members, and 35 have just one. Merging them into about 34 makes browsing much faster. The concept directory shows how that looks.

3. Facebook audit

10 items, 2 urgent. Low engagement, missing contact info

Checked facebook.com/PartnershipLH on October 30, 2026.

What’s working

  • 8K followers.
  • A strong, on-brand cover photo and logo.
  • A clear mission in the intro.
  • Heavy use of Facebook Events.
  • 90% recommend the chamber (19 reviews).

Gaps

  • Fix Lots of posts, little engagement. About 50 posts in 30 days, but most get 1-8 likes, 0 comments and 0-1 shares. The best performers are people posts: We’re Hiring (21 likes, 9 comments, 7 shares), Ambassador of the Month Jamarcus Charles (25, 3, 3) and Ambassador Todd Byers (14, 2, 3).
  • Fix Mostly text-heavy flyers, reposted often. The same flyer goes up many times; the Veterans Luncheon flyer has been posted about 6 times since Oct 2.
  • Fix No Page replies seen. Comments (including a job seeker’s comment on the hiring post) and reviews have no reply from the Page.
  • Polish Low event RSVPs. Most events show 1-5 people going or interested.

Match the website

  • Urgent Contact info. Only Messenger is listed. Add the phone, (281) 446-2128, and the email, chamber@lakehouston.org.
  • Urgent Hours. None are listed; the Page only shows “Closed now.” Add office hours to match Google: Monday-Friday, 8:30 AM-5 PM.
  • Polish Address format. The Page shows “110 W Main St, Humble, TX, United States, 77338.” The website shows “110 West Main Street, Humble, TX 77338.” It’s the same address, so pick one format and use it on both.
  • Fix Event locations. The Dec 15 Elected Officials Breakfast Reception event lists the location as just “Humble,” but the flyer says Humble Civic Center. Use the full venue name on events.
  • Polish Past events. The Economic Development Conference (Fri, Oct 2) still shows on the Page. Clear or archive past events.
  • Fix Business & Bacon date. The featured post says “October 1,” but the event shows “Fri, Oct 2.” Double-check which date is correct; they may be two different things.

Good news: the website errors (the 11:0 AM time, CDT vs CST, and the LakeHosuton email) don’t appear on Facebook. The Facebook posts for those events are correct.

4. Google Business Profile audit

5 items, none urgent. Strong rating, quiet profile

What’s working

  • The profile exists, with the Chamber of Commerce category.
  • 4.7 stars (14 reviews).
  • The correct phone number, (281) 446-2128, plus the website and hours (Monday-Friday, 8:30 AM-5 PM).

Gaps

  • Fix No recent Google Updates. No posts were seen on the profile.
  • Polish No Q&A. No questions and answers were seen.
  • Fix Reviews go unanswered. Only one owner reply was seen, about 7 years old. Newer reviews have no reply.
  • Fix Few reviews. Only 14 reviews for a chamber with 8K Facebook followers.
  • Fix Hours don’t match Facebook. Google has Monday-Friday, 8:30 AM-5 PM. Copy these hours to Facebook so they match.

5. Social strategy

30-day plan: 8 steps, plus 2 sample posts
  1. Post less, but better. At most 1 flyer per event per week, and about 4-5 posts a week overall.
  2. Use a weekly mix. 1 member spotlight, 1 event recap with real photos, 1 short video or Reel (the podcast teaser format), 1 event promo and 1 community or share post.
  3. People over flyers. Show faces and names, and tag member businesses. Tags extend reach to members’ followers.
  4. Reply within 24 hours. Answer every comment and review from the Page.
  5. Ask for reviews. After every event, ask members with a short Google review link and a QR code on table tents. Goal: 25+ reviews on each platform.
  6. Keep Google active. Post every event as a Google Update, add fresh photos monthly, and add 5 common Q&As: membership cost, how to join, ribbon cuttings, office hours and parking.
  7. Build each event once. Create the Facebook Event once, then share it. Ask ambassadors to RSVP and share it in the first 24 hours.
  8. Track it monthly. Reach, engagement rate, comments, reviews and event RSVPs.
