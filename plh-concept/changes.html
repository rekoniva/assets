Where things went:

  • Testimonials and Partnership Opportunities 2027 are now on the Membership page.
  • Bizcom, Business & Bacon and Chamber on Tap share one Networking Programs page.
  • Luncheons are part of Signature Events.
  • Leadership Lake Houston moved to Community.
  • Contact is its own menu item.
  • Lake Houston Deals moved under Membership.

Homepage: shorter and easier to scan

  • A photo at the top with the Join button.
  • A short “Who We Are” with a link to the full story on About.
  • The next 3 events, with a link to the full calendar.
  • The video plays only when someone taps it.
  • The Elite Investors logos are cropped so they read clearly.

Every page: one template, built for phones first

  • The same header, page banner, spacing and footer on every page. The header stays at the top while you scroll.
  • Designed for a phone screen first, then desktop. No page scrolls sideways.
  • Buttons and links are big enough to tap easily.
  • Site search covers pages, events, people and member businesses.
  • Long sections fold open, and photo groups become swipeable slideshows.
  • Staff, Board, Ambassadors, Contact, Deals, the Event Calendar and the Member Directory are rebuilt on the same template.

Pages hidden or merged, and why

  • Volunteer: lakehouston.org/volunteer/ shows “Page not found,” so there was nothing to bring over.
  • Industries: the page is 5 tiles that open Word documents rather than web pages, so it’s left out until those become pages.
  • Properties: the page is the property map tool, so the footer links straight to that tool.

2. Fixes worth making on lakehouston.org

Listed by impact: broken links first, then wrong information, then typos.

Broken: messages or visitors don’t get through

  1. Bizcom sponsorship email is misspelled. On lakehouston.org/bizcom/ the line reads “Sponsorships are available by emailing interest to SMagee@LakeHosuton.org”. “Hosuton” isn’t the chamber’s domain, so those emails won’t reach you. The correct address, smagee@lakehouston.org, is on the very next line.
  2. Industries tiles open Word documents in a personal OneDrive folder. On lakehouston.org/industries/, all 5 tiles (Energy Technology, Life Sciences, Logistics, Advanced Manufacturing, Corporate Operations) link to .docx files on a personal OneDrive account rather than to web pages. Visitors may be asked to sign in.
  3. The Volunteer page is missing. lakehouston.org/volunteer/ shows “Page not found.”

Wrong information

  1. Veterans Appreciation Luncheon time. The homepage (lakehouston.org/) reads “11:0 AM - 1:00 PM (CST)”. The event calendar has it right: 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM.
  2. Sheldon Bizcom time zone. The homepage lists Thursday, November 12 as “11:00 AM - 12:00 PM (CDT)”. Daylight saving time ends November 1, so this should read CST.

Typos

  1. Our Elite Investors, Generation Park (lakehouston.org/our-elite-investors/):
    • “spend countries hours” → “spend countless hours”
    • “enable those of our clients” → “enable our clients”
    • “For its the employees” → “For it’s the employees”
  2. Our Elite Investors, Memorial Hermann: “for 40 years Recognized” is missing a period after “years.”
  3. Testimonials (lakehouston.org/testimonials/): “priceless. .” has an extra period.

Worth tidying

  1. Member Directory categories. The directory (members.lakehouston.org/active-member-directory) has 106 categories. 56 of them have only 1 or 2 members, and 35 have just one. Merging them into about 34 makes browsing much faster. The concept directory shows how that looks.

3. Facebook Page suggestions

Small edits to facebook.com/PartnershipLH so it matches the website:

  • Add the phone number, (281) 446-2128, and the email, chamber@lakehouston.org, to the Page’s contact info.
  • Add office hours.
