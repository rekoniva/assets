Our Elite Investors

STAKEHOLDERS

The city of Humble is home to some of the best-hidden gems in Texas. Located only 20 minutes from downtown Houston, Humble has a diverse community and offers a number of amenities to residents and newcomers. From beautiful golf courses to public facilities, the city of Humble has continued to offer a thriving atmosphere for businesses, big or small, since 1933.

Generation Park, one of the world’s largest privately-owned commercial developments, is transforming the face of Houston, Texas as we know it. With a mission to enable those of our clients, we set out to do just that by creating a place that celebrates the employee. For its the employees who make up the businesses. People with needs, emotions, and desires. It is for these people we spend countries hours designing and redesigning places, spaces, and the master plan to create an environment where people can thrive. Because when people thrive, businesses thrive.

Memorial Hermann Northeast Hospital has been caring for families in the greater Lake Houston Area for 40 years Recognized nationally as a leader for patient safety and quality, it is advancing health and providing the greater Lake Houston community access to world-class care close to home. As part of the Memorial Hermann Health System, the largest not-for-profit health system in Southeast Texas, Memorial Hermann Northeast is integrated with a network of 14 acute care hospitals and numerous specialty programs.

CHAMPION

Insperity has been a trusted advisor to America’s best business since 1986. Insperity provides an array of human resources and business solutions designed to help improve business performance, delivering administrative relief, better benefits, reduced liabilities, and a systematic way to improve productivity. Whether looking for full-serve HR or an individual HR solution. Insperity has what you need.

CATALYST

With a visit to Deerbrook Mall in the Greater Houston Metro area, you’ll check everything off your list. This one-stop retail center features more than 130 shops and a collection of the most popular anchor department stores, making it the preferred shopping destination in the region, they offer a wide variety of entertaining activities, a movie theater, a variety of dining options, and more.

As a full-service, 419-bed acute-care hospital, HCA Houston Healthcare Kingwood continues to provide Northeast Houston communities with high-quality, cost-effective healthcare for more than 25 years. HCA Houston Kingwood has more than 500 physicians on staff, supported by 1,500 plus healthcare professionals, and offers a range of inpatient and outpatient medical, surgical, and specialty services. We believe a quality patient experience is central to fulfilling our mission to the care and improvement of human life.

San Jacinto College is focused on student success, academic progress, university transfer, and employment. It is committed to opportunities that enrich the quality of life in the communities it serves. today nearly 30,000 young men and women find their path at San Jacinto College. They nurture their aspirations with faculty mentors who know real-world success and apply deep career expertise in more than 185 academic and technical programs.

Lone Star College System welcomes 97,000+ students each semester providing high-quality, low-cost academic transfer and career training education. LSCS is training tomorrow's workforce today and redefining the community college experience to support student success.

GROWTH

AlphaGraphics is a premier marketing and printing solution serving the Humble market with a focus on results and the return-on-investment of business marketing dollars. As a local visual marketing and communication expert, they truly care about product quality, customer satisfaction, and establishing long-term partnerships with every client.

Arbor Care, Inc. is a Houston-based professional, affordable, and fully insured Tree Service specialist. They provide quality shade tree pruning and tree removal, along with several other tree services. With 9 ISA Certified Arborists on staff, Arbor Care, Inc. has the skills and experience to embark on providing state-of-the-art techniques for the best tree care service.

Berkeley Eye Center is recognized as one of Texas’ most established comprehensive eye care facilities. In addition to 23 clinics and optical locations located throughout the greater Houston area, they offer patients the added access and convenience of five state-of-the-art laser eye centers throughout Texas, and the Caplan Surgery Center, a Medicare-licensed ambulatory surgery center for outpatient cataract care.

Chimichurris is the epitome of “simple chic” with modern design elements and the best flavors of South America. Their cruise combines the freshest seasonal ingredients with an exclusive line of spices and techniques. The interior compliments the cuisine creating a celebration of the virtues of great taste in a warm atmosphere – the perfect blend of style, service, elegance, and comfort.

Costco Wholesale Corporation is an American multinational corporation that operates a chain of membership-only big-box retail stores. As of 2020, Costco was the fifth largest retailer in the world, and the world’s largest retailer of choice and prime beef, organic foods, rotisserie chicken, and wine as of 2016.

DNA Studios started in Humble, Texas. In only a few years they’ve provided entertainment and sports programming for millions of viewers on television and the web. Their desire isn’t to be the biggest company, merely the best. Regardless of whether the client is a small local business in Kingwood or the client is “The Worldwide Leader in Sports”, DNA has earned a reputation of delivering the goods on time, on budget and realizes that failure is NOT an option..

With locations throughout the Greater Houston area, Houston Methodist Primary Care Group is dedicated to providing quality patient care for the entire family. Houston Methodist Primary Care Group is proud to be a part of Houston Methodist and its family of hospitals, ensuring efficient access to specialty and hospital services whenever the need arises.

Humble Independent School District is a school district located in Humble, Texas, United States. It serves the city of Humble, small portions of the city of Houston (including the community of Kingwood), and portions of unincorporated Harris County (including the communities of Atascocita and Fall Creek). Humble ISD has become one of the fastest-growing school districts in Texas with nearly 43,553 students enrolled in the years of 2018-2019.

IDS provides professional engineering and surveying services to a wide variety of public and private clients. Established in 1970, they are 100% employee-owned and are registered to offer and perform civil engineering services by the State of Texas and the Texas Board of Professional Land Surveying. IDS professionals provide experience and in-depth knowledge of the markets we serve including the regulatory bodies that govern those markets.

Since 1951, JSL Foods has been a leading manufacturer and marketer of Asian Noodles and Wrappers, Long Goods Pasta, and Baked Goods. They aim to deliver on-trend foods with superior ingredients, manufacturing equipment, innovative processes, and a deep commitment to Research and Development.

Pioneer Plumbing & Septic was established in 2009 and offers a number of plumbing and septic services to the Houston community. They have an entire team of licensed plumbers, registered apprentices, and qualified dispatch and office personnel who are committed to performing every job with integrity and professionalism.

Service Wire Co. is a second-generation, family-owned wire and cable manufacturer. They are committed to producing the finest quality American-made products while providing industry-leading service levels. Their nationwide network of distributors and advanced distribution capabilities enable them to meet and exceed the often complex needs of industrial, commercial, utility, pump, irrigation, and transit customers throughout North America and the world.

Tachus is an internet provider that provides 100% fiber-optic services to communities in the North Houston areas of Atascocita, Conroe, Kingwood, Oak Ridge North, and The Woodlands. Since 2018, they have been focused on providing fast, reliable Internet that requires no explanation.

Formerly known as Cardiovascular Association, Vital Heart & Vein was founded in 1998 by a team of highly trained cardiologists and vein specialists in Houston, Texas dedicated to providing genuine care to patients. Their heart and vein specialists provide a full spectrum of diagnostic testing, comprehensive therapies, and interventional/electrophysiologic procedures utilizing the latest technology.

Waste Management, Inc. is a waste management, comprehensive waste, and environmental services company based in North America. Founded in 1968, the company is headquartered in the First City Tower in Houston, Texas. They provide a range of services to both residential and commercial properties.
